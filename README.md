## ⚡ who i am

B.Tech CSE (IoT) @ **Shiv Nadar University Chennai**. I build systems that talk to databases, queues, sockets, hardware sensors, and each other — simultaneously.

I care about **architecture over syntax**. Whether it's a multi-provider AI fallback pipeline, a graph-based fraud detector, or a BLE-connected bottle cap — I want the system to actually work under pressure.

Currently: interning at **VVDN Technologies**, co-building **RunOne** (social fitness for run clubs), and always looking for the next hard problem.
